# P0.4 — World map model provenance
27 GLB derivatives from CC0 OpenGameArt Quaternius source packs: Q05 village structures and props, Q01 five humanoid NPC models, Q08 plants/crops, Q13 tiled roofs. Exact member, scale, triangles, actions and textures in conversion.json. Source archives and license evidence in assets/manifest.csv and docs/p00; originals untouched. Conversion Blender 4.3.2 using tools/p00/convert_pilot.py; Q13 roof meshes fitted to stone buildings via scale_xyz in layout, gable structural panels generated separately. Existing menu GLBs are referenced without duplication; not a new character rig. Q01 NPC anatomy/rig is a scene archetype; bespoke faces, role outfits and interactions are pending P0.6, avatar modularity P0.5. No player equipment is permanently displayed.
Terrain, road ribbons, plaza paving, gable fills and dock planks/posts are original structural surfaces, not substituted decorative assets. Godot water shader reused. GLB imports can generate texture PNGs in local editor, excluded from Git.
