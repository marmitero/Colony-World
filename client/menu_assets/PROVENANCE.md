# P0.2 — animated title menu asset provenance
All GLBs except boat are byte-identical to P00 derived QA samples in prototypes/p00/assets, tracked by docs/p00/pilot_manifest.csv. P00 visual direction approved 27/11/2026. These copies are menu-only; not a promotion to full runtime gameplay. GLBs are self-contained; Godot may extract textures locally; never commit extracted copies.
- boat.glb: Q09 Quaternius, LowPoly Ship Pack, BoatWSail.fbx, normalized 4.2 m with P00 Blender converter. CC0 internal document in original; not vessel gameplay.
- theme.mp3: M08, codemanu, A Legend Will Rise (Orchestral), original audio CC0 per OpenGameArt page.
- ambience.mp3: M03, tinyworlds, Forest Ambience, original audio CC0 per OpenGameArt page.
- click.wav: S01 artisticdude RPG Sound Pack, interface/interface1.wav (CC0 page), button sound.
See assets/manifest.csv for original source URL and SHA-256. No proprietary game assets. Menu harbor, UI, lighting, water shader and motion are original composition.
